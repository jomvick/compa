## THE LONG-TERM VISION

Compa's stated destination — not a maybe, a committed direction — is to become
an **open-source community project**. Once the core (V1–V3: a living, purely
emotional companion) is proven and stable, Compa opens up through **domain
packs**: optional, community-buildable extensions that let a user turn their
Tux toward a chosen domain (coding, fitness, focus...).

Domain packs are allowed to make Compa genuinely useful, not just decorative.
This is a deliberate evolution of the mission, not a contradiction of it.

Two things stay non-negotiable while this happens:

1. **The core (V1–V3) never changes shape.** No dashboard, no data, no
   productivity surface ever enters the base experience described in RULE #8.
   Usefulness lives exclusively in opt-in domain packs, never in the base Tux.
2. **Domain packs express usefulness through Tux, not around him.** A domain
   pack changes Tux's vocabulary, triggers, moods and accessories — never adds
   a panel, a chart, or a list on top of him.

The exact permissiveness of community-made domain packs (how much real
functionality they're allowed to carry, what a "bonus" is allowed to look
like) is intentionally left open for now and will be formalized before V4
begins — not before.
